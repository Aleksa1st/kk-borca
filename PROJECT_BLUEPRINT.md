# KK Borča Digital Platform — Project Blueprint

Version: 1.0  
Platform name: Borča Basketball Academy  
Club: KK Borča  
Language: Serbian Latin  

---

## Mission

Razvijamo decu kroz košarku.

Basketball is our tool. The goal is to help children grow as players, students, teammates and responsible young people.

---

## Vision

KK Borča will become a modern youth basketball club that connects sport, education, family, community and digital technology.

---

## Core Message

Svako dete zaslužuje priliku da raste.

Više od košarke.

---

## Design Direction

70% professional.  
30% energetic and playful.

The website must create:

- trust for parents
- excitement for children
- confidence for partners

---

## Main Users

### Children / Players
Want to train, have fun, improve and belong to a team.

### Parents
Want safety, trust, communication and visible progress.

### Coaches
Need simple tools for attendance, statistics and player development.

### Partners
Want to support children and see real community impact.

## Important Facts

- Founded in 1996
- Around 160 active players
- 8 competition teams last season
- 5 coaches
- 25 training sessions per week
- Training in 4 sports halls
- Strong girls' youth results
- U13 and U15 girls reached Belgrade Final Four two years in a row
